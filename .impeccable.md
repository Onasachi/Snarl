## Design Context

### Users
People playing a short puzzle game on a phone, including first-time players who need
to learn by dragging a bead. Their goal is to untangle creatures and collect them in a
pond, with daily and weekly puzzles giving them reasons to return.

### Brand Personality
Calm, friendly, playful. The existing creator-supplied references in `reference/` and
the continuation brief establish the direction: creatures are the reward and should
own the space. Copy should explain the next action in plain language.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the established beads-and-strings drawing style, Fredoka typography, night and
paper themes, and soft water motion. Use the warm accent for crossings. Preserve the
centre-first pond layout and compact daily/weekly tiles from the accepted redesign.

### Design Principles
- Get players to a real creature quickly; teach with the game itself.
- Keep the pond visible while explaining the next step.
- Make identity and the complete wallet address easy to find.
- State daily rewards clearly and make the game deliver them reliably.
- Respect reduced motion, keyboard access, and small phone screens.
